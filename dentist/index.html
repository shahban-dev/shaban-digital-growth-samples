FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Do you take new patients? +

Yes — new patients are welcome, and we usually have same-week appointments. Book online or call (555) 014-2200.

Will it hurt? +

Most check-ups and cleans are painless. For anything else we numb the area first and go at your pace — tell us if you need a break, any time.

Do you offer payment plans? +

Yes. We explain the full cost before treatment and can split larger treatments into monthly payments. Ask us when you book.

What if I have a dental emergency? +

Call us as early as you can in the morning. We hold same-day slots for toothache, chips, and lost fillings.

Where do I park? +

Free parking is behind the clinic on Maple Street, with step-free access from the car park to reception.
